The short version

  • We help you find a job. We don’t promise you’ll get one.

Who we are

Shortlist (“we”, “us”) provides job-search support: CV and LinkedIn rewriting, applications made on your behalf with your approval, and interview preparation. Registered business details will be listed here once confirmed.

What we do and don’t do

We provide career support focused on international job applications. We are not a recruitment agency, we don’t place candidates, and we don’t take fees from employers. Hiring decisions belong to employers.

We don’t arrange visas or provide immigration services, and we don’t guarantee employer sponsorship or visa approval. Any sponsorship and immigration decisions are subject to employer requirements and the relevant authorities.

No guarantee of employment

We do not guarantee interviews, job offers, employment, sponsorship or visas. We guarantee only the services and deliverables expressly stated in your service agreement.

Suitability assessment

All packages are subject to an initial suitability assessment on your free intake call. We may recommend a different package, recommend the Readiness Package first, or explain honestly that we are not the right fit.

Your approval

We only send applications to roles you have approved in your tracker or in writing. You can pause or stop applications at any time.

Accurate information

You agree to give us truthful information. We will not invent or exaggerate experience, qualifications or references, and we may pause work if information turns out to be untrue.

Fees and payment

Your package, price, duration and payment terms are fixed in your service agreement, which you review and sign before paying. Payments are processed by Paystack. We never see or store your card details.

Cancelling

See our refund policy for how cancellations and refunds work for each package.

Liability

We’ll deliver our services with reasonable care and skill. We aren’t responsible for employers’ decisions, delays or the content of their job adverts.

Law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.
