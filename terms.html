Who we are

Shortlist (“we”, “us”) provides job-search support: CV and LinkedIn rewriting, applications made on your behalf with your approval, and interview preparation. Registered business details will be listed here once confirmed.

What we do and don’t do

We are a job-search support service. We are not a recruitment agency, we don’t place candidates, and we don’t take fees from employers. Hiring decisions belong to employers.

No guarantee of employment

We do not guarantee interviews, job offers or any salary. We guarantee the work listed in your service agreement: the documents, the number of applications and the preparation sessions.

Your approval

We only send applications to roles you have approved in your tracker or in writing. You can pause or stop applications at any time.

Accurate information

You agree to give us truthful information. We will not invent or exaggerate experience, qualifications or references, and we may pause work if information turns out to be untrue.

Fees and payment

Prices are shown on our website and fixed in your agreement before you pay. Payments are processed by Paystack. We never see or store your card details.

Cancelling

See our refund policy for how cancellations and refunds work for each package.

Liability

We’ll deliver our services with reasonable care and skill. We aren’t responsible for employers’ decisions, delays or the content of their job adverts.

Law
