Why people come to us

Finding a job abroad shouldn’t feel like guesswork.

You’ve worked hard to build your skills and experience. But applying for jobs in another country can feel like starting from scratch.

  • Your CV isn’t getting results.

    You need to present your experience in a way that aligns with the expectations of employers in your target market.

  • Finding suitable jobs takes time.

    Searching across job boards, checking requirements and completing applications can quickly become overwhelming.

  • You’re unsure which opportunities are realistic.

    Not every vacancy, employer or country will be a suitable match for your background.

  • Interviews feel unfamiliar.

    You want to communicate your experience clearly and prepare for the expectations of international hiring managers.

  • You don’t know what’s happening with your applications.

    You need a clear record of where you’ve applied and what happens next.

You don’t have to manage every part of the process alone.

At Shortlist, we help you approach your international job search with a clear strategy, professional application support, and honest guidance.

    How it works

    Six steps, from first call to clear progress.

    Some steps are yours, some are ours, and a few we do together. You sign before you pay.

    YouUsTogether
    1. Step 1You

      Book your free intake call

      Tell us about your experience, qualifications, preferred roles and target countries. We’ll discuss your goals and assess whether our service is a suitable fit.

    2. Step 2Us

      Receive an honest assessment

      We’ll explain whether we can help, what your options are and which package best fits your needs. If we don’t believe we can offer meaningful support, we’ll tell you.

    3. Step 3Together

      Agree on your service

      If you’re accepted, you’ll receive a written service agreement setting out your package, deliverables, duration and terms. Review and sign it before making payment.

    4. Step 4You

      Provide your information

      Submit your CV, work history, qualifications and other relevant details through our secure process. Together we agree your target roles, countries and application criteria.

    5. Step 5Us

      We get to work

      We start delivering the services in your package. Depending on your selection, this may include CV optimisation, job searching, application submissions and interview preparation.

    6. Step 6Together

      Track your progress

      Follow your application activity through your progress tracker and receive updates according to your package.

    What we do, and what we don’t

    We provide career support focused on international job applications. We don’t guarantee jobs, sponsorship or visas.

    Employers make their own hiring decisions. Any sponsorship and immigration decisions are subject to the relevant employer requirements and authorities.

    We do

    • Search for relevant job opportunities
    • Improve your CV and, where included, your LinkedIn profile
    • Submit agreed applications on your behalf
    • Run mock interviews and give feedback where included
    • Record application activity and provide agreed updates

    We don’t

    • Guarantee job offers, interviews or employment
    • Guarantee employer sponsorship or visa approval
    • Arrange visas or immigration services
    • Invent qualifications, experience or achievements
    • Apply to roles outside your agreed criteria
    • Promise access to employers or vacancies we can’t verify
