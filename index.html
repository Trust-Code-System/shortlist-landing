    How it works

    From first call to first interview.

    Six steps. Some are yours, some are ours, and a few we do together.

    YouUsTogether
    1. Step 1You

      Book the intake call

      Twenty minutes on video. We ask about the roles you want, your salary range, where you want to work and what hasn’t worked so far.

    2. Step 2You

      Sign and pay online

      Read the service agreement, sign it in your browser and pay by card or bank transfer. The agreement says exactly what we deliver, and by when.

    3. Step 3You

      Upload your CV and work history

      Send your current CV, certificates and a short form about each past role. Rough notes are fine. Turning them into strong lines is our job.

    4. Step 4Together

      We rewrite, you approve

      Your first draft arrives within five working days. Comment on any line. Nothing is sent to an employer until you approve it.

    5. Step 5Us

      Applications go out

      We shortlist roles that match your brief, tailor each application and send it. Every one shows up in your tracker with the date and status.

    6. Step 6Together

      Prepare for the interview

      When an employer replies, we run a mock interview built around that role and send notes on what to tighten.

    Your tracker

    Every application, on the record.

    No “we’re working on it” emails. Log in at any time to see which roles we applied to, when we applied and what happened next.
