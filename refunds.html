Refund policy

Last updated 9 October 2026Version 0.1
Draft. This text is a placeholder and will be reviewed by a lawyer before Shortlist launches.

The short version

  • You receive this policy before you pay, and it matches your service agreement.
  • Before we start work on your package, you can cancel for a full refund.
  • Once work has started, any refund reflects the work already delivered.
  • Refunds go back to your original payment method.

Before work starts

If you cancel after paying but before we start work on your package, we refund the full amount.

After work has started

Once work has started, any refund is calculated on the deliverables still outstanding in your service agreement. Completed work, such as a delivered CV rewrite, is not refundable.

If we don’t deliver

If we don’t deliver what your agreement lists within the stated service period, tell us. We’ll either complete the work or refund the difference.

Hiring outcomes

Refunds are not available because an employer did not invite you to interview or offer you a job, or because sponsorship or a visa was not granted. We don’t guarantee these outcomes.

Pausing instead

If you need a break, ask your specialist to pause applications. Your service period can be paused once by agreement.

How to ask for a refund

Email hello@shortlist.ng within 14 days of the payment, with your name and the payment date. Approved refunds are paid through Paystack to your original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 working days.
