Refund policy

Last updated 8 October 2026Version 0.1
Draft. This text is a placeholder and will be reviewed by a lawyer before Shortlist launches.

The short version

  • CV Rewrite: full refund until we start your first draft.
  • Monthly plans: cancel any time before your next billing date and you won’t be charged again.
  • The current month isn’t refunded once its applications have started.
  • Refunds go back to your original payment method.

CV Rewrite (one-off)

You can cancel for a full refund until we start your first draft. Once work has started, refunds are at our discretion and pro-rated to the work already delivered.

Active Search and Full Concierge (monthly)

Cancel any time from your tracker or by email. Cancelling stops future payments. The current month is not refunded once applications for that month have started. If no applications have gone out yet that month, we’ll refund it in full.

If we don’t deliver

If we don’t deliver what your agreement lists for a month, tell us. We’ll either complete the work or refund the difference.

Pausing instead

You can pause applications from your tracker. You won’t be charged while paused, and you can restart whenever you’re ready.

How to ask for a refund

Email hello@shortlist.ng within 14 days of the payment, with your name and the payment date. Approved refunds are paid through Paystack to your original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 working days.
