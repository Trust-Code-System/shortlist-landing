Job-search support agreement

This agreement is between Shortlist (“we”, “us”) and (“you”) for the package.

1. What we will do

2. What we don’t promise

We do not guarantee that you will be invited to interview or offered a job. Employers make their own decisions. We are a job-search support service, not a recruitment agency, and we do not take fees from employers.

3. Your approval

We will not send any application, CV or message to an employer that you have not approved. You can approve or reject roles from your tracker or by message.

4. Your responsibilities

You agree to give us accurate information about your experience and qualifications. We will not invent or exaggerate experience on your behalf, and we may pause work if information turns out to be untrue.

5. Fees and payment

6. Cancelling and refunds

7. Your documents and data

Your CV, certificates and personal details are stored privately and shared only with employers you have approved. You can ask us to delete them at any time, and we will confirm when it’s done.

8. Ending this agreement

Either of us can end this agreement with written notice by email. Work already delivered stays yours.

9. Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

End of agreement.
