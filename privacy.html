Legal

Privacy notice

Last updated 9 October 2026Version 0.1
Draft. This text is a placeholder and will be reviewed by a lawyer before Shortlist launches.

The short version

  • We collect what we need to run your job search, and nothing for advertising.
  • Your CV is shared only with employers you approve.
  • We never see your card details. Paystack handles payments.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time.

What we collect

  • Contact details: name, email, phone or WhatsApp number.
  • Career information: your CV, work history, certificates, target roles, salary expectations and preferences.
  • Service records: bookings, your signed agreement, approvals, messages and application statuses.
  • Payment status from Paystack. We do not receive or store card numbers.

How we use it

To deliver the services in your agreement: rewriting your documents, finding and applying for roles you approve, preparing you for interviews and keeping your tracker up to date. We don’t sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Employers you have approved, when we apply on your behalf.
  • Service providers who help us run Shortlist, such as payments (Paystack), email and calendar tools, under contracts that protect your data.
  • Authorities, only if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

While you’re a client, and for 12 months after your last activity so you can come back to your tracker. Ask us and we’ll delete it sooner, unless we’re legally required to keep a record (for example, payment records).

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask to access, correct, move or delete your personal data, and object to how we use it. Email privacy@shortlist.ng and we’ll reply within 30 days.

Security

Documents are stored privately, access is limited to your specialist and the people who need it, and connections are encrypted.

Cookies

We only use cookies needed to run the site and keep you signed in. No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.
