About this page

  • This is the standard agreement every client signs at checkout.
  • Your signed copy, with your name, package, price and the date you signed, is emailed to you and kept in your tracker.

1. What we will do

Deliver the services listed for your package (Readiness, Starter, Standard or Full Service), which may include an international CV rewrite, LinkedIn optimisation, a personalised job search, agreed applications submitted on your behalf, a progress tracker, weekly updates and mock interviews, as stated in your agreement.

2. What we don’t promise

We do not guarantee interviews, job offers, employment, employer sponsorship or visa approval, and we do not provide visa or immigration services. Employers make their own hiring decisions. We are a career support service, not a recruitment agency, and we do not take fees from employers.

3. Your approval

We will not send any application, CV or message to an employer that you have not approved. You can approve or reject roles from your tracker or by message.

4. Your responsibilities

You agree to give us accurate information about your experience and qualifications. We will not invent or exaggerate experience on your behalf, and we may pause work if information turns out to be untrue.

5. Fees and payment

The fee for your package is stated in this agreement, paid in advance after you sign, and processed by Paystack. The service period for your package is stated in this agreement.

6. Cancelling and refunds

As set out in our refund policy.

7. Your documents and data

Your CV, certificates and personal details are stored privately and shared only with employers you have approved. You can ask us to delete them at any time. See our privacy notice.

8. Ending this agreement

Either of us can end this agreement with written notice by email. Work already delivered stays yours.

9. Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.
